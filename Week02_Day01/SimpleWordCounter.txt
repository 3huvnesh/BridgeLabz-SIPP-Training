Pseudo-code: Simple Word Counter

Method countWords(paragraph)
    Split paragraph into words
    Convert each word to lowercase
    Remove punctuation
    For each word
        If word in map
            map[word] += 1
        Else
            map[word] = 1
    Sort map by frequency
    Print words with counts

Main
    Paragraph = "The news today is about the rise in technology trends."
    Count words → {the:2, news:1, today:1, ...}
